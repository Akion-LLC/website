For Legislators & Drafters

Waypoint

Automated reference intelligence for legislative drafting

When drafting or reviewing legislation, assembling every relevant reference, precedent, and statutory conflict requires significant manual effort. Waypoint eliminates that burden — surfacing the complete reference picture automatically so legislators can focus on applying their judgment, not finding documents.

  • Automated tracking of every statutory reference within a bill
  • Conflict detection against existing law and pending legislation
  • Side-by-side amendment comparison with full change history
  • Complete citation resolution with source document retrieval
  • Collaborative drafting environment with version control

For Advocates & Lobbyists

PolicyUnboxed

Precision bill monitoring with configurable filter rules

Policy professionals need to stay current across hundreds of active bills without drowning in noise. PolicyUnboxed monitors legislative activity across all jurisdictions and delivers only what matches your rules — defined with precision, not left to algorithmic guesswork.

  • Rule-based monitoring across federal and all 50 state legislatures
  • Filter by topic, sponsor, committee, jurisdiction, status, or keyword
  • Real-time alerts on bill status changes, new filings, and amendments
  • Digest and briefing report generation for stakeholder communications
  • Team workspaces with shared rule sets, annotations, and history
