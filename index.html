Policy, structured for machines.

Bills, regulations, hearings, and ordinances are scattered across every level of government. Akion turns them into one versioned data layer that Sentinel's agents read, compare, and reason over.

  1. 01
    Ingest every level
    Congress, the Federal Register, state legislatures, and county and town boards, plus transcribed hearing recordings so what's said on the record is searchable too.
  2. 02
    Version every change
    Every amendment and regulatory revision is diffed against the last, so "five" becoming "three" megawatts never slips past.
  3. 03
    Reason about your clients
    Each change is matched against your clients' issues and projects and explained in terms of what it means for them, not just that something moved.
